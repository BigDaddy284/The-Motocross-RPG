🧰 Motocross RPG Toolkit

Toolkit instructions Play the game

The editors for building the game's tracks, maps and, later, outfits and characters. Each one opens in its own page. They need a computer with a mouse and keyboard, and work best in Chrome or Edge.

Editors

🏁 Track Editor Race tracks: the route, start straight, sections, jumps, surfaces, ground, hills, scenery, paddock vehicles and trackside items, edited in the game's own race view. Saves mod tracks the game can race. Ready 🧊 Models The race view's 3D objects: export any of them, the race bike and the rider as .glb files for Blender, and see which of your own models in public/mods/models the game uses, or why not. Ready 🗺 Map Editor The areas you walk and drive round: paint tiles, collision and props, and place spawns, exits, doors and block zones. Its Tiles tab draws new tiles and its Buildings tab builds buildings, pictures and all. Ready

Coming later

🧥 Outfit Designer Rider clothing and riding gear: jerseys, pants, helmets, boots and gloves, their colours and designs, for the player and the CPU riders. Coming later 🧑 Character Designer The people of the world: NPC and rider looks, from body and hair to the sprites the game draws on the street and in the paddock. Coming later

Using the toolkit

  • The full guide to every editor, mode by mode, is Toolkit instructions (docs/TOOLKIT_INSTRUCTIONS.md in the repo); each editor's Help button has the short version.
  • On the published site, open an editor here and use Open folder… to pick your copy of the game's files.
  • On your own copy, run npm run dev and open localhost:5173/tools/. The track editor can then load and save tracks straight from the game's folders.
  • The game itself doesn't link to the toolkit, so players on a phone never see it.
